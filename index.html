What this means in practice

Free & open source

Every project ships under an OSI-approved license — mostly GPLv3, some MIT. Read the source, fork it, patch it, build it yourself. No asking permission.

De-Google

No Play Services dependency where it can be avoided, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporters phoning home, no ad identifiers. What the code does is what the app does — nothing else runs in the background.

Local-first, sovereign data

Apps work fully offline and keep data on your device by default. When they sync, it happens over protocols you control — not into a database someone else owns, monetizes, or can flip off.

Full control of the device

From an offline photo gallery to a full automotive dashboard, the goal is the same: your phone runs what you choose, not whatever a vendor preinstalled or quietly discontinued.

Bitcoin & Nostr

Money and identity as open protocols, not products. Self-custody over Bitcoin, censorship-resistant identity and sync over Nostr — no custodian, no platform to de-platform you.
